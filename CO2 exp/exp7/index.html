About Hogwarts Academy

Welcome to the Hogwarts Academy Quiz Challenge, a magical learning experience inspired by the enchanting world of wizardry. This interactive quiz portal combines fun, fantasy, and knowledge through spell-based questions, magical creatures, house competitions, and exciting achievements.

Players choose their Hogwarts House, earn House Points, unlock magical badges, and climb the leaderboard while testing their knowledge. The project demonstrates HTML, CSS, JavaScript Arrays of Objects, Event Handling, Automatic Score Calculation, Responsive Design, and Interactive UI concepts in an engaging way.
