About Saveetha University

Saveetha Institute of Medical and Technical Sciences (SIMATS), commonly known as Saveetha University, is one of India's leading multidisciplinary universities committed to academic excellence, innovation, research, and holistic student development. Located in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, the university offers world-class education through modern infrastructure, highly qualified faculty, advanced laboratories, and strong industry collaborations.

The university provides an inspiring learning environment where students are encouraged to think creatively, conduct research, and develop practical skills that prepare them for successful global careers. With a student-centric approach, Saveetha focuses on innovation, entrepreneurship, and social responsibility.
