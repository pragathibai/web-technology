Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register for an event?

Select an event and click the Register button. Complete the registration form and submit your details.

Can I register for multiple events?

Yes. Students can participate in multiple events, provided the event schedules do not overlap.

Is event registration free?

Most university events are free. Events requiring registration fees will clearly display the applicable fee.

Where can I find the event venue?

The venue and event date are displayed on every event card before registration.
